HOW TO PLAY MATH SHOP

01

Read the Shopping List

Check the shopping task at the top (e.g. "Buy 2 Apples and 1 Milk") to see required items.

02

Pick Products From Shelves

Click items on the shelves to add to your basket. Use + or - to adjust quantities.

03

Check Register Total (RM)

The register calculates the total price in Malaysian Ringgit (RM) for all items in your basket.

04

Click RM notes (RM50, RM20, RM10, RM5, RM1) and coins (50c, 20c, 10c, 5c) to match the total exactly!
