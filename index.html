HOW TO PLAY — SUPERMARKET SHOPPING

01

Read the Shopping List

Check the shopping task at the top (e.g. "Buy 2 Apples and 1 Milk") to see which items and quantities to collect.

02

Pick Products From Shelves

Click items on the supermarket shelves to place them into your shopping basket. Use + or - to adjust quantities.

03

Check the Register Total (RM)

The cash register calculates the total price in Malaysian Ringgit (RM) for all items in your basket.

Click RM notes (RM50, RM20, RM10, RM5, RM1) and sen coins (50¢, 20¢, 10¢, 5¢) to match the total. Pay accurately to earn points and stars!
